This device at IPPLM Warsaw is one of the largest plasma focus machines in the world. Designed for intense neutron emission and soft X-ray research, the PF-1000 uses a massive capacitor bank to drive a plasma sheath through a coaxial electrode geometry, culminating in a high-density "pinch" phase where nuclear fusion occurs at high temperatures.
