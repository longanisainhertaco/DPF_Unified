# DPF Plasma Color Science

## Real Deuterium Emission Colors
- D-alpha (Balmer-alpha): 656.3nm → #FF0000 (red)
- Fulcher-alpha band: 580-640nm (peak 606nm) → #FF5500 (orange-red)
- D-beta (Balmer-beta): 486nm → #00FFFF (cyan, weak)

## Blackbody Color Equivalents
| Energy | Kelvin | Color | Hex |
|--------|--------|-------|-----|
| 1 eV | 11,600K | Warm white-yellow | #FFEC92 |
| 10 eV | 116,000K | Blue-white | #8AADFF |
| 100 eV | 1.16MK | UV (not visible) | — |

## Renderer Phase Colors (v8 Cycle 18)
- Rundown: D-alpha red-orange [1.0, 0.33, 0.0]
- Radial: orange-red [1.0, 0.55, 0.10]
- Pinch: warm white-yellow [1.0, 0.92, 0.57] (1 eV blackbody)
- Post-pinch: cooling red-orange [0.85, 0.30, 0.05]
